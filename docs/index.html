Murmur · Privacy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

Murmur is a private voice-messaging app for two people. It is built so that your messages stay yours. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

The short version

  • We do not collect, see, or store your data on our servers. Murmur has no servers of its own.
  • Your voice messages and transcripts live in your own private iCloud account and are shared only with the one person you invite.
  • Transcription happens entirely on your device. Your audio is never sent to us or any third party.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs.

What Murmur stores, and where

  • Voice recordings and transcripts are saved locally on your iPhone and synced through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud container. Only you and the partner you invite can access them. The developer cannot read them.
  • Your profile (your name, your partner's name, a colour, and time-zone preference) is stored locally on your device.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

  • The microphone is used only while you are recording a murmur.
  • Speech Recognition transcribes your murmurs on-device. Audio is not uploaded for transcription.

Sharing

Murmur shares your murmurs with exactly one person: the partner you invite via Apple's secure CloudKit sharing. There is no public content, no discovery, and no group messaging.

Data retention and deletion

Your murmurs remain in your iCloud until you delete them in the app or remove the app's iCloud data from your device settings. Because the data lives in your iCloud, you are in control of it at all times.

Children

Murmur is intended for personal use between two adults and is not directed at children.

Questions about your privacy? Email support@murmur.app.
